Our belief

People should keep the judgement. Everything else is negotiable.

Contract software spent twenty years digitising the filing cabinet. It got very good at storage and never touched the work itself — the reading, the checking, the chasing, the decisions made at eleven at night because someone needed an answer before a call in the morning.

So we built the other half. Agents that sit inside the record rather than beside it in a chat window, doing the reading and the chasing, speaking for the people they represent, and stopping precisely where judgement begins.

And because an agent you cannot audit is an agent you cannot trust, every single thing the AI does here is logged, attributable and reversible. Prompt version, confidence, latency, outcome. One table. One click to undo.

That is the whole idea. Spend the day on the two percent that actually needs you.
