What it does

Four things
nobody else does
this well.

Not a chat box bolted onto a repository. These are the places where the AI actually carries the work — and where you can still see exactly what it did.

01

Conversational intake

Describe what you need in plain language. The assistant asks only what is missing, fills every field with provenance and a confidence score, scores your precedents, predicts the routing, and assembles the draft from your templates and playbook. Drop in third-party paper and it reads that instead.

02

Agent Crew

Every participant on a contract can have an agent that speaks for them in the discussion — in the first person, grounded in the record. Ask a question and it routes itself to whoever is best placed to answer: finance, legal, the approver. Agents advise. People still decide.

03

Auto-rejection

Approvers describe a gate in their own words. The AI restates it as a numbered list of executable requirements — attachment, field and text checks — that you can dry-run against a live task before it is ever allowed to fire. Bad requests stop before they reach your queue.

04

Portfolio inquiry

Ask your portfolio a question in one line. You get the filtered register, the AI's restatement of what it understood, editable filters when it read you wrong — and a chart you can pin straight to your dashboard.

See it work on your own paper.

Bring a real contract and a real approval chain. Thirty minutes, live, no slides — intake to obligation.
