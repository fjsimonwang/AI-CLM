Every team

One platform.
Six sets of problems.

Contracts are never one team's job. The platform holds the whole path end to end — and shows each person only the slice they are cleared for.

Stop being the bottleneck.

Playbook tiers, clause variants and AI document review turn every request into a decision you can make in minutes rather than an afternoon of reading.

  • Deviations are quoted, ranked by severity and written straight to the risk register.
  • Paste counterparty language and get its closest playbook tier with the reasoning.
  • An approver briefing on every task: what changed, what deviates, what is being asked of you.

Vendor paper, on your terms.

Third-party paper stops being a black box. Upload it and the platform reads the document, fills the record and checks it against your playbook before an approver ever opens it.

  • Supporting documents travel with the request — including through a revise-and-resubmit.
  • Counterparty master data carries type and sanctions status.
  • Auto-rejection rules can require the diligence pack before the request reaches you.

Value you can actually see.

Signing authority, thresholds and contract value roll up into a register you can filter, sort, chart and pin — with expiry and renewal on one pipeline.

  • Per-column filters and sorts on the register; your layout persists.
  • Signing authority modelled per entity, user, type and maximum value.
  • Save any inquiry as a live dashboard chart.

Ship the deal.

One request, plain language, a few minutes. No form to decode, no guessing which template applies, no waiting to find out who has to sign it.

  • Start from a recent contract: new one like this, same party new type, or amend it.
  • The expected routing and its score are shown before you submit.
  • Returned request? Revise and resubmit updates the same contract — same number, no duplicates.

Obligations don't slip.

Every obligation is extracted with its source quote and a confidence score, then owned, dated, verified and closed out — with overdue in red where nobody can miss it.

  • Low-confidence extractions are queued for human verification, not silently trusted.
  • Policies and procedures are tagged by role, country and region — the assistant answers from them.
  • Every AI interaction lands in a governance log you can filter and revert.

Family trees, detected.

Amendments, masters and statements of work rarely arrive labelled. The platform proposes the links and waits for a human to confirm them.

  • AI-detected relations arrive with a confidence score and its reasoning.
  • Confirm or dismiss — nothing is linked behind your back.
  • Migrated contracts are badged, with unverified low-confidence terms surfaced up front.

See it work on your own paper.

Bring a real contract and a real approval chain. Thirty minutes, live, no slides — intake to obligation.
